  • Bengoh Dam

    Bengoh Dam, located about 45 km from Kuching in Padawan, Sarawak, is both a vital water reservoir and a hidden eco‑tourism gem. It’s best known as the gateway to stunning waterfalls like Curtain, Pe’an, and Susung, and the nearby Bidayuh village Kampung Sting, which offers homestays and cultural experiences.

  • Semenggoh Wildlife

    Semenggoh Wildlife Centre, just 24 km south of Kuching, is Sarawak’s most famous orangutan rehabilitation reserve, where around 30–40 semi‑wild orangutans roam freely in 650 hectares of rainforest. The best time to visit is during the daily feeding sessions (9–10 AM and 3–4 PM), when sightings are most likely.

  • Bako National Park

    Bako National Park is one of Sarawak’s most iconic destinations — and the oldest national park in the state, gazetted in 1957. Despite its small size (about 27 sq km), it packs in an incredible diversity of landscapes and wildlife.
